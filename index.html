Synthetic World Values Survey — Exploratory Analysis

The data analysed on this page is entirely synthetic (simulated): every row was fabricated to mirror the distributions and relationships reported in the real World Values Survey, Wave 7 (Haerpfer, C., Inglehart, R., Moreno, A., Welzel, C., Kizilova, K., Diez-Medrano J., M. Lagos, P. Norris, E. Ponarin & B. Puranen (eds.). 2022. World Values Survey: Round Seven – Country-Pooled Datafile Version 5.0. Madrid, Spain & Vienna, Austria: JD Systems Institute & WVSA Secretariat. worldvaluessurvey.org), which this dataset is modelled on. No real respondent data is used anywhere here, and every finding below is illustrative only — it describes patterns in the simulation, not real-world results.

Respondents are simulated from five countries across Asia: China (East Asia), India (South Asia), Singapore (Southeast Asia), Turkey (Middle East), Kazakhstan (Central Asia).
